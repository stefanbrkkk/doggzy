# Question Tree — Shelters, Rescuers, Vets/Clinics, Donors, Adopters, EU‑funded Projects

Purpose: Guide conversations from broad to specific, so we consistently gather comparable, decision‑ready information. Use this as a branching flow. Pick the audience and follow the relevant subtree. Keep calls to 15–30 minutes. Capture structured fields per `partner-info-schema.md` and log in `../trackers/partner-info.csv`.

Sources informing this tree (illustrative, non‑exhaustive):
- NIH OACU — Guidelines for Standards of Care in Animal Shelters (medical records, vaccination, intake protocols): https://oacu.oir.nih.gov/system/files/media/file/2021-02/shelterguide.pdf
- ASPCA — Position Statement on Responsibilities of Animal Shelters (care, placement, accountability): https://www.aspca.org/about-us/aspca-policy-and-position-statements/position-statement-responsibilities-animal-shelters
- ICAM Coalition — Shelter design and management guidelines (capacity, disease control, SOPs): https://www.icam-coalition.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Shelter-guidelines-English.pdf
- PMC — Animal shelters and animal welfare: Raising the bar (funding, staffing/training, euthanasia/intake pressures): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3398531/
- European Commission — Eligibility: who can get funding?: https://commission.europa.eu/funding-tenders/how-apply/eligibility-who-can-get-funding_en
- European Commission — Funding of Animal Health Measures: https://food.ec.europa.eu/horizontal-topics/funding-procurement-grants/food-chain-funding/funding-animal-health-measures_en

## Top Priority Questions (use across audiences; adapt wording)
1) Mission and current focus: What are your top 2–3 priorities for the next 6–12 months?
2) Capacity and constraints: What limits your impact most today (e.g., funding, staff/volunteers, vet access/costs, space, transport)?
3) Cost ranges: What are your typical monthly costs (food, medicine, vet care, transport, other)? Any recent spikes?
4) Vet access and pricing: Which clinics do you use? Typical costs for common treatments? Is ambulance/transport available?
5) Adoption flow and challenges: How do you screen, what’s hardest, what would improve outcomes?
6) Transparency and updates: If donors fund a case, what updates and evidence can you provide, and how often?
7) Data and consent: Any sensitivities with media/receipts? What are your privacy constraints?
8) Willingness to pilot: Would you pilot update cadences and evidence standards? What cadence is realistic?
9) Verification: Are vet confirmations or receipts feasible for treatments? Any blockers?
10) Warm intros: Who else should we speak with (2–3 names) and could you intro us?

## A) Shelter / Rescuer Question Tree
- A1. Background
  - A1.1 Size today: dogs in care, avg intake/month, adoption rate.
  - A1.2 Team: staff count, volunteers, training; key SOPs (intake, isolation, cleaning, vaccination).
  - A1.3 Facilities: space, quarantine area, storage, transport.
- A2. Needs & Costs
  - A2.1 Top needs (rank 1–3) and drivers.
  - A2.2 Monthly cost ranges: food, medicine, vet, transport, utilities, other.
  - A2.3 Buffer/shortfalls: seasonal peaks, emergencies.
- A3. Veterinary Access
  - A3.1 Clinics used; coverage radius; ambulance availability.
  - A3.2 Typical pricing: vaccinations, deworming, spay/neuter, wound care, x‑ray, hospitalization.
  - A3.3 Billing terms; whether receipts/attestations can be provided.
- A4. Adoption & Virtual Adoption
  - A4.1 Intake→adoption funnel; screening steps; contracts.
  - A4.2 Virtual adoption interest; acceptable update cadence; visit policy and safety.
  - A4.3 Common blockers (behavioral, medical, legal, transport).
- A5. Transparency & Evidence
  - A5.1 What updates can you realistically provide per funded case? (text, photos/videos, receipts, vet confirmations)
  - A5.2 Cadence options: first update within X days, then monthly/bi‑weekly; what’s feasible?
  - A5.3 Constraints: bandwidth, consent, privacy; redaction needs.
- A6. Funding & Reporting
  - A6.1 Current sources: individual donors, grants (incl. EU), corporate, shop; restrictions.
  - A6.2 Reporting obligations to funders (frequency, data required, audit readiness).
- A7. Systems & Readiness
  - A7.1 Current tools (spreadsheets, CRM, messaging); what would help most?
  - A7.2 Willingness to try simple mobile forms for updates; batch uploads.
- A8. Referrals
  - A8.1 Who should we talk to next? Request 2–3 intros.

Branching prompts:
- If costs are uncertain → ask for ranges and last month’s example.
- If receipts are hard → ask what substitutes are feasible (photos, vet notes) and redaction options.
- If adoption bottlenecks are severe → ask for top 3 cases and root causes.

## B) Veterinary Clinic / Ambulance Question Tree
- B1. Services & Coverage
  - B1.1 Services offered; after‑hours/emergency; ambulance.
  - B1.2 Coverage area; typical response times; capacity constraints.
- B2. Pricing & Billing
  - B2.1 Price ranges for common procedures (vaccination, spay/neuter, wound care, diagnostics).
  - B2.2 Billing and discounts for shelters/rescuers; documentation provided (receipts, attestations).
- B3. Partnership & Verification
  - B3.1 Willingness to verify treatments for donor transparency; process and SLAs.
  - B3.2 Data/consent: what is acceptable to share; anonymization.
- B4. Operations & Risks
  - B4.1 Common medical issues from shelters; protocols to reduce disease transmission.
  - B4.2 Constraints (equipment, staffing, medicine shortages).
- B5. Referrals
  - B5.1 Other clinics/ambulances to speak with; potential coverage gaps.

## C) EU‑Funded or Grant‑Funded Project Leads (Animal Welfare)
- C1. Programme Basics
  - C1.1 Grantor and programme type (EU/other); co‑financing requirement; duration; budget.
  - C1.2 Eligibility path and key compliance checkpoints (procurement, state‑aid, ethics, data protection).
- C2. Reporting & Evidence
  - C2.1 KPI and evidence expectations; verification sources; audit trail requirements.
  - C2.2 Financial reporting cadence; timesheets; procurement records; asset registers.
- C3. Risk & Governance
  - C3.1 Fraud, conflict‑of‑interest, sanctions/AML checks.
  - C3.2 Data/privacy for media; consent management; retention.
- C4. Lessons Learned
  - C4.1 Biggest blockers to disbursement; common audit findings.
  - C4.2 Advice for small orgs starting out.
 
Prompts to go deeper (EU context):
- Procurement: thresholds, quotes/tenders required, documentation expectations; any national rules that also apply.
- Co‑financing: typical % covered vs. own match; eligibility of in‑kind; cash flow timing.
- Eligibility of costs: what is considered direct vs. indirect; depreciation/equipment rules; VAT treatment.
- Verification: what counts as sufficient evidence for animal welfare outcomes (e.g., vet attestations, receipts, geo/time‑stamped media).
- Data protection: handling photos/videos of dogs/people; consent forms; retention and access.
- Monitoring visits/audits: what do they look for; common pitfalls that delay payments.
