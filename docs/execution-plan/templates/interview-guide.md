# Interview Guide (Shelters, Vets/Clinics, Rescuers, Adopters, Donors)

Purpose: Consistent interviews to gather facts for Serbia-first investigation. Pick the relevant section per audience. Keep interviews 15–30 minutes.

## General Instructions
- Record date, org/person, role, contact, consent to use non-PII quotes.
- Ask open-ended; avoid leading; probe for evidence and examples.
- Capture metrics whenever possible (counts, costs, timeframes).

---

## Section A — Shelters / Rescuers
- Background: size, dogs in care, intake rate, adoption rate.
- Needs: top 3 needs (funding, food, medicine, vet care), monthly costs (ranges).
- Vet access: availability, pricing, ambulance/transport.
- Adoption process: screening, contracts, challenges.
- Transparency & updates: ability to provide donor follow-ups (cadence, evidence types), constraints.
- Virtual adoption: interest, visit logistics, safety, consent.
- Tech/tools used today; what would make life easier.
- Willingness to pilot; MOU expectations (update cadence, evidence).

## Section B — Vets / Clinics / Ambulances
- Services offered and pricing ranges; geographic coverage.
- Common medical cases for shelter dogs; cost ranges.
- Willingness to partner; billing/verification process.
- Evidence feasibility: invoices, medical notes, photos; consent.
- Ambulance/transport availability and protocol.

## Section C — Adopters / Posters
- Current adoption journey (where, how, timeline, blockers).
- Info needed to adopt with confidence; pain points.
- Virtual adoption expectations (update cadence, media, visit policy).
- Data/consent comfort levels (photos/videos).

## Section D — Donors (one-time/recurring)
- Motivation and barriers to donating; importance of transparency.
- Reaction to specific-case vs general donations; subscriptions.
- Preferred update cadence and channels (email/SMS/push).

## Wrap-up
- Ask for referrals to 2–3 other contacts.
- Permission to follow up with clarifying questions.
