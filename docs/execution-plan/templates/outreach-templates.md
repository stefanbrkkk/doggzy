# Outreach Templates (Email/DM)

Purpose: Speed up scheduling of interviews with shelters, vets/clinics, rescuers, adopters, and donors. Personalize lightly for each contact.

## Shelter/Rescuer — Initial Outreach
Subject: Partnering to help dogs in Serbia — 15–20 min intro call

Hello <Name>,

We’re building a Serbia-first, transparent portal to help dogs — donations (with follow-ups), adoption & virtual adoption, and a knowledge base. We’d love your perspective on needs and challenges.

Could we schedule a 15–20 min call this week? We’ll keep it simple and share findings back with you.

Topics: funding gaps, vet access/costs, adoption process, and feasibility of posting updates (photos/receipts) for donors.

Thank you,
<Name> — <Role/Org>
