# Initial High‑Level Plan: Dog Welfare Organization & Web Portal

This document captures the full, professional documentation stack needed to launch a dog‑welfare organization and web portal whose mission is to:
- Run a shop where 100% of profit goes to shelters, helping dogs, and running the portal
- Enable donations (general and specific to shelters/people/dogs)
- Provide comprehensive adoption support (for people posting dogs for adoption and for prospective adopters)
- Offer "virtual adoption" (sponsor a specific dog in a shelter with regular updates and optional visits)
- Publish a comprehensive, country‑by‑country knowledge base (starting with Serbia) and be the one‑stop portal for: buying food/accessories, finding veterinary ambulances/clinics, training and care guides, what to know before adopting, etc.
- Create and sell our own products (dog and owner accessories) in the shop
- Support subscription/recurring donations (e.g., monthly for a specific dog or shelter)
- Build a network of shelters, veterinarians, people, and organizations
- Maintain a flexible, modular program model so more programs can be added over time
- Design the platform to be reusable for other causes (e.g., people in need, cats, wild animals)
- Provide transparent histories for each dog, shelter, and case, and donor follow‑ups showing how funds were used; surface these timelines and statistics on entity pages

Initial market focus: Serbia, with a country‑by‑country expansion model.

Use this as the master checklist and outline for drafting each artifact, aligning stakeholders, and tracking progress to launch.

## 4) Impact and M&E (Monitoring & Evaluation) Framework

- **KPIs and outcome metrics**

  - Donor follow‑up KPIs: % of donations with at least one follow‑up update, median time‑to‑first‑update, update cadence adherence, % updates with verified evidence

  - State/province fundraising registrations, solicitation disclosures (country‑specific)
- **Payments & donations**

  - Update obligation policy tied to donations and virtual adoptions (expected cadence, content standards); disclosure in T&Cs
- **Privacy & data protection**
  - GDPR/CCPA compliance; privacy policy; data processing agreements
  - Data retention schedule; parental consent if minors ever involved in content
- **Terms of Service & Acceptable Use**
  - Donor T&Cs; shelter/vet partner T&Cs; adoption and virtual adoption terms; content/community guidelines
  - Update and evidence submission terms; media rights and consent for updates
- **IP & content**
  - Copyright, licensing of user‑submitted photos/videos; DMCA process
- **International considerations**
  - Cross‑border donations, sanctions/OFAC screening, export controls for aid
- **Shop compliance**
  - Sales tax/VAT registration, consumer rights, returns/refunds, product safety labels

## 9) Product and Technology Plan
- **Product strategy & roadmap**
  - MVP scope: donation module (general/specific, recurring), adoption support (list, manage, and discover dogs; guidance for posters and adopters), virtual adoption features (updates, media, visit coordination), shop (incl. our own products), content CMS/knowledge base, vet/clinic directory, basic shelter/vet profiles, transparency pages
  - Phased releases: community features, case updates, impact dashboards, advanced adoption matchmaking, moderation tools
- **Architecture & platform choices**
  - CMS (e.g., headless CMS) for knowledge base; e‑commerce (Shopify/WooCommerce/custom) for shop + own products; donation processors (Stripe/PayPal/Donorbox) with recurring; CRM (Salesforce NPSP/HubSpot), analytics
  - Modular, program‑centric architecture to add new programs easily; multi‑tenant/multi‑vertical readiness (future: people in need, cats, wild animals)
- **Data model and workflows**
  - Entities: dogs, cases, adoption listings, shelters, vets, donors, donations, subscriptions, virtual adoption relationships, shop products/orders
  - History timeline events per entity (type, timestamp, description, media, evidence), donation→update linkage, donor notification preferences, public timelines on dog/shelter pages
  - Specific‑case funding flow and status updates; adoption messaging and screening workflows; virtual adoption update cadence
  - Audit/versioning of updates; verification status tracking
- **Security and privacy**
  - AuthN/Z, PII minimization, encryption, audit logs, secrets management
- **Accessibility and localization**
  - WCAG 2.1 AA; Serbian language/currency at launch; framework for additional locales; region‑specific vet/clinic directories
- **Uptime/monitoring and incident response**
  - SLOs, error budgets, on‑call playbook
- **Vendor due diligence**
  - DPAs, SOC 2/ISO references, data residency
