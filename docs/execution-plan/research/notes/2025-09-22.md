## Key Findings (facts with sources)
- Training & SOPs are critical; funding shortfalls cascade into welfare issues. Source: PMC “Animal shelters and animal welfare: Raising the bar” (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3398531/)
- Accurate medical records, vaccination protocols, and isolation reduce disease burden; core vaccines and intake protocols are baseline. Source: NIH OACU “Guidelines for Standards of Care in Animal Shelters” (https://oacu.oir.nih.gov/system/files/media/file/2021-02/shelterguide.pdf)
- Shelters’ responsibilities include quality care and placement, with policies balancing discretion and accountability. Source: ASPCA Position Statement (https://www.aspca.org/about-us/aspca-policy-and-position-statements/position-statement-responsibilities-animal-shelters)
- Management/design guidelines emphasize capacity planning, hygiene, and disease control SOPs. Source: ICAM Guidelines (https://www.icam-coalition.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Shelter-guidelines-English.pdf)
- EU programme considerations: eligibility, co‑financing/match, procurement rules, verification/evidence, data protection, and audit expectations. Sources: EC eligibility (https://commission.europa.eu/funding-tenders/how-apply/eligibility-who-can-get-funding_en), EC Animal Health Measures (https://food.ec.europa.eu/horizontal-topics/funding-procurement-grants/food-chain-funding/funding-animal-health-measures_en)
