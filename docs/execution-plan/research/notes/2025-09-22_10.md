## Findings (with citations)

### 1) Laws & Authorities
- Law on Animal Welfare — `Zakon o dobrobiti životinja (Sl. glasnik RS 41/2009)` (consolidated text available): defines welfare, responsibilities, prohibited acts, oversight and sanctions.
  - Reference: https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_dobrobiti_zivotinja.html
- Veterinary Law — `Zakon o veterinarstvu (Sl. glasnik RS 91/2005, 30/2010, 93/2012, 17/2019)`; municipalities may be required to ensure quarantine/holding; framework for veterinary supervision, animal health protection, and public services.
  - Reference: https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_veterinarstvu.html
- Competent authority — `Uprava za veterinu` (Veterinary Directorate) under Ministry of Agriculture: publishes forms, certificates, movement rules, and guidance (including pet movement to/from Serbia).
  - Reference (movement hub): https://www.vet.minpolj.gov.rs/medjunarodni-promet-i-sertifikacija/kretanje-kucnih-ljubimaca/

### 2) Municipal Zoohigijena (public services)
- Belgrade — `JKP Veterina Beograd`: public veterinary/zoohygiene operator; pages show communal zoohygiene function and contact; operates acceptance centres and sterilization campaigns.
  - Reference: https://www.veterinabeograd.rs/latn/komunalna-zoohigijena
- Novi Sad — `Zoohigijena Novi Sad`: has acceptance centre (`Prihvatilište`) with basic info, services, and contact.
  - Reference: https://zoohigijenans.co.rs/prihvatiliste-za-zivotinje/

### 3) Pet Movement / Adoptions to EU (Serbia → EU)
- Germany (official guidance): Serbia is a third country not on the EU listed group for simplified pet entry; entry of dogs/cats/ferrets into EU requires microchip (ISO), valid rabies vaccination, and — for non‑listed countries — `rabies neutralising antibody titre test (RNATT)` before entry; limits for non‑commercial movements (≤5 animals); commercial moves follow TRACES/CHED‑A regime.
  - Reference: German Embassy in Belgrade guidance (Serbian page): https://belgrad.diplo.de/rs-sr/service/2368296-2368296
- EU legal base for pet travel/movement: Regulation (EU) No 576/2013 and Implementing Regulation (EU) No 577/2013 (applies to non‑commercial movement of pet animals; sets certificate, identification, RNATT, and listing of countries). Rescue group transports often fall under commercial rules requiring TRACES.
  - Reference (EU explainer): https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/carry/pets-and-other-animals/index_hr.htm
- Serbian authority page for pet movement (forms/certificates):
  - Reference: https://www.vet.minpolj.gov.rs/medjunarodni-promet-i-sertifikacija/kretanje-kucnih-ljubimaca/

### 5) Funding Landscape (priority)
- EU Delegation to Serbia — Direct management calls (civil society, human rights/democracy). Animal welfare is not always a stand‑alone lot, but CSOs may apply with thematic projects (advocacy, education, transparency, local partnerships). Registration via PADOR/PROSPECT.
  - Calls portal: https://europa.rs/eu-funding/?lang=en
  - Example 2024–2025 call (civil society): https://europa.rs/new-grants-call-support-for-human-rights-democracy-and-civil-society-in-serbia/?lang=en
- IPA (Instrument for Pre‑Accession Assistance) — Sector support sometimes includes `animal health and welfare` projects (mostly institutional capacity, Veterinary Directorate, disease control). While not typical NGO operating grants, they can open partnership opportunities (pilots, verification systems, training).
  - Serbia IPA overview: https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/enlargement-policy/overview-instrument-pre-accession-assistance/serbia-financial-assistance-under-ipa_en
  - Example fiche “Support to Food Safety & Animal Welfare”: https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/document/download/a840425e-fcc8-4be5-9852-697ee2b406c5_en?filename=20_support_to_food_safety_animal_welfare.pdf
- Foundations and domestic donors (potential fits depend on call focus):
  - `Trag fondacija` — domestic foundation with grant programmes for CSOs and civic initiatives; animal welfare projects sometimes fit under community development/education/environment.
    - https://tragfondacija.org/en/
  - Grant aggregators/directories for Serbian CSOs (cross‑thematic, useful to discover embassy/foundation calls):
    - Civic Initiatives funding guide: https://vodic.gradjanske.org/konkursi/
    - Donors directory: https://vodic.gradjanske.org/donatori/
- Embassies (small grants — thematic varies by year; look for community, education, environment, youth, civil society):
  - U.S. Embassy Serbia (Democracy Commission Small Grants and other programmes): https://rs.usembassy.gov/
  - Other embassies occasionally run small grants; check embassy sites and the above directories for active calls.
- International animal‑welfare funders (project or material support):
  - SPCA International — Shelter Support Fund (some Serbian shelters featured): https://www.spcai.org/our-work/shelter-support-fund/
  - IFAW — International Fund for Animal Welfare (programme‑based, global): https://www.ifaw.org/international
  - Eurogroup for Animals — policy network (not a typical grant maker, but relevant for advocacy alignment): https://www.eurogroupforanimals.org/

### 6) Adoption/Export Programmes (Serbia→EU)
- Anecdotal and NGO‑led programmes send Serbian dogs to adopters in Germany/other EU states. Compliance typically requires: microchip, rabies vaccine, RNATT (for Serbia as non‑listed), EU health certificate by competent veterinarian, waiting periods, and — for organised transports — TRACES/CHED‑A and registration as a transporter/operator. Expect additional destination‑country specifics (e.g., Germany state‑level measures).
  - Core refs: German Embassy guidance, EU pet movement page, Serbian Veterinary Directorate movement page (links above).
