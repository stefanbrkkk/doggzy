# Research Notes — Serbia→EU Dog Adoption / Transport Compliance — 2025-09-22_12

## Header
- Date: 2025-09-22
- Focus: Practical compliance checklist for moving/adopting dogs from Serbia to EU (EN + SRB sources)
- Note: Serbia is a non-listed third country for pet entry. Rescue transports often qualify as commercial movements.

## Quick Definitions
- Non‑commercial movement: Owner (or authorised person) moves ≤5 pets, not for sale/transfer of ownership.
- Commercial movement: Any transfer of ownership or organised transport (shelter→adopter), or >5 animals, or movements not meeting non‑commercial criteria. Requires TRACES/CHED‑A and operator registration.

## Non‑Commercial Movement Checklist (pet with owner)
- Microchip (ISO 11784/11785) implanted before vaccination.
- Rabies vaccination (in line with product SPC; primary vaccine at ≥12 weeks of age).
- RNATT (rabies neutralising antibody titre test) at EU‑approved lab — required for non‑listed countries like Serbia. Sample ≥30 days after vaccination.
- Waiting period: 3 months from the date of blood sampling before entry into EU.
- EU Health Certificate (multilingual model), issued by the competent Serbian authority veterinarian shortly before travel (usually validity ~10 days for entry).
- Max 5 animals; same itinerary; owner/authorised person accompanies.

## Commercial Movement (Rescue/Adoption Transports)
- Operator/establishment registration as per Serbian and EU rules (transporter/dispatching body).
- TRACES NT account; issuance of CHED‑A by competent authority at entry.
- Pre‑movement clinical exam and official certificate (commercial model).
- Same animal ID, rabies vaccine, RNATT + 3‑month wait apply.
- Transporter authorization and vehicle standards; journey logs as applicable.

## Destination-Specific Notes (Germany example)
- Federal states may apply additional rules (e.g., breed restrictions, quarantine where documents are incomplete). Always check state veterinary office guidance.
- Customs/entry checks can refuse animals without complete documentation; fines/return possible.

## Evidence to Capture (for our transparency timelines)
- Microchip number and implantation date (photo of reader display/label).
- Vaccine sticker (batch/lot) and date; passport/certificate page scan.
- RNATT lab report (date of sampling, result ≥0.5 IU/ml, lab stamp).
- Official certificate (non‑commercial or commercial model) — scan/photo.
- TRACES/CHED‑A reference for commercial movements.

## Risks / Common Pitfalls
- Attempting travel before the 3‑month wait after RNATT sampling.
- Using non‑approved labs for RNATT.
- Misclassifying a rescue transport as non‑commercial.
- Missing/illegible microchip documentation.
