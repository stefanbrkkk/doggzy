## Practical Funding Strategy (for Serbia animal welfare NGOs)
- Map calls by horizon: EU Delegation (CSO windows), embassy small grants, domestic foundation rounds (Trag), international animal‑welfare funds (SPCAI/IFAW).
- Prepare compliance package (for grants and donor transparency): governance docs, financial procedures, KYC, consent/media policy, update SOPs.
- Build partner coalitions (municipal zoohigijena, clinics, universities) — increases credibility for EU/embassy calls.
- Track opportunities in a rolling spreadsheet; subscribe to newsletters (EU Delegation, Civic Initiatives, Trag, SPCAI/IFAW).
