# Research Notes — Funders Deep Dive & Monitoring Process (Serbia) — 2025-09-22_13

## Header
- Date: 2025-09-22
- Scope: Specific funders and how to monitor/apply; focus on Serbia animal welfare NGOs and adjacent themes (civil society, environment, education, transparency).
- Method change: Lightweight URL capture only (no heavy page fetching) to avoid IDE UTF‑8 issues. All links are authoritative or well‑known hubs.

## EU — Delegation to Serbia (Direct Management)
- Calls dashboard and applicant guidance (PADOR/PROSPECT registration required):
  - https://europa.rs/eu-funding/?lang=en
- Example CSO call 2024–2025 (Human Rights/Democracy/Civil Society):
  - https://europa.rs/new-grants-call-support-for-human-rights-democracy-and-civil-society-in-serbia/?lang=en
- Practical notes
  - Eligibility: Serbian CSOs usually eligible; check lots/priorities.
  - Budget size: typically mid-to-large (six figures+); co‑financing often required (10–20% typical, confirm per call).
  - Evaluation: relevance, methodology, capacity, budget realism, sustainability; partnerships add strength.
  - Monitoring routine: subscribe to EU Delegation newsletter; check page weekly; set Google Alert “site:europa.rs call for proposals Serbia”.

## EU — IPA (Pre‑Accession)
- Country page with links to actions, project fiches, and key docs:
  - https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/enlargement-policy/overview-instrument-pre-accession-assistance/serbia-financial-assistance-under-ipa_en
- Example fiche clearly mentioning Animal Health & Welfare (institutional support):
  - https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/document/download/a840425e-fcc8-4be5-9852-697ee2b406c5_en?filename=20_support_to_food_safety_animal_welfare.pdf
- Practical notes
  - IPA frequently funds institutions (Veterinary Directorate) rather than NGO operating grants; however, NGOs can appear as partners/subcontractors (training, verification pilots, transparency tools).
  - Monitoring: follow “EU za tebe” project portal for Serbia-wide projects — https://www.euzatebe.rs/en/

## Monitoring Process (Step‑by‑Step)
1. Subscribe to newsletters: EU Delegation (europa.rs), Civic Initiatives (vodic.gradjanske.org), Trag fondacija, SPCAI/IFAW.
2. Weekly scan (15 minutes):
   - `europa.rs/eu-funding` recent posts
   - `vodic.gradjanske.org/konkursi` new calls
   - Embassy pages quick check (bookmarks)
3. Maintain a simple pipeline sheet with fields: funder, call name, link, deadline, theme fit, co‑fin %, eligible entities, rough budget, required partners, decision date, status.
4. Prepare reusable annexes: governance docs, financial procedures, consent/media policy, update SOPs (ties to donor‑follow‑up requirement), basic data protection policy.
5. Build a partner consortium list (zoohigijena, clinics, universities, NGOs) with warm intros for rapid coalition forming.
