# Partner Pre‑Brief — Before We Talk

Purpose: Align on what we’re building, what we’ll ask from partners, and how we handle transparency, consent, and effort — so conversations are efficient and respectful.

## Who We Are (Short)
- Mission: Transparent, specific‑impact support for dogs in Serbia and beyond.
- What we do: donations (with donor follow‑ups), adoption & virtual adoption, knowledge base, and a shop where 100% of profit funds care.

## What Makes This Different
- Every dog/shelter/case has a public history timeline (events, media, receipts where applicable).
- Donors receive follow‑ups tied to their donation (what was done, with evidence where possible).
- KPIs we track publicly: % donations with follow‑up, time‑to‑first‑update, cadence adherence, % updates with verified evidence.

## What We Ask From Partners (Pilot Expectations)
- Timely updates for funded cases and virtual adoption (cadence to be agreed; e.g., first update within X days, then monthly/bi‑weekly).
- Evidence when feasible: photos/videos; receipts; vet confirmations (for treatments).
- A single point of contact to streamline communication.
- Consent: confirmation that individuals appearing in media have consented; avoid sensitive content without explicit permission.

## What We Offer to Partners
- Visibility to donors and adopters; storytelling support.
- Tools and templates for updates (simple to use; low overhead).
- Potential in‑kind support (via partners) and access to vetted clinics.
- Training on update standards; reminders and escalation help.

## Consent, Privacy, and Safety
- We minimize PII and avoid sensitive data unless necessary and consented.
- Media rights: partners confirm the right to share images/videos.
- Redaction: we can blur faces/plates and redact receipts if needed.
- Safety first: visit policies for virtual adoption will follow clear guidelines.

## Transparency & Evidence Standards (v0)
- Update types: text, photos/videos, receipts, vet attestations.
- Not every update needs all evidence; we aim for a balanced, realistic standard.
- We mark updates with verification status (unverified/partner‑verified/vet‑verified).

## Time & Effort Expectations
- We keep admin light: mobile‑friendly forms; batch uploads.
- Update SLAs are agreed in the MOU; reminders are automated.

## What We Need to Know From You (Discovery Call)
- Your main needs (food/medicine/vet/transport) and monthly ranges.
- Adoption flow (screening, contracts, challenges).
- Current ability to provide updates and evidence; constraints to consider.
- Vet relationships and any preferred clinics/ambulances.
- Interest in pilots; best way to collaborate.

## Boundaries & Non‑Goals (For Now)
- We won’t publish sensitive medical details without consent.
- We won’t require burdensome paperwork; receipts are sufficient when available.
- We won’t promise guaranteed funding volumes during pilot.

## Next Steps After the Call
- If aligned: we follow up with a short MOU draft (includes update cadence and evidence guidelines).
- We’ll schedule a simple onboarding and first case listing.
- You’ll receive templates, timelines, and a contact channel.
