# Execution Plan — Investigation-First Strategy

Purpose: Execute an investigation-first program to build authoritative, Serbia-first knowledge and partnerships before heavy tech build. Like in a strategy game: reveal the map first, then expand with precision.

## Objectives & Outcomes
- Comprehensive understanding of Serbia’s dog-welfare and adoption ecosystem.
- Verified legal/compliance path and risks for fundraising, adoption, payments, privacy.
- Initial network of shelters, vets/clinics, rescue groups, and advisors.
- Clear GTM angles, messaging, and content pillars informed by facts.
- Evidence-based inputs to the Business Plan, Marketing Plan, Financial Model, and Legal Pack.

## Principles
- Investigation before implementation (facts > assumptions).
- Primary sources (interviews, official regs) over secondary when possible.
- Lightweight tech exploration only to de-risk decisions; build later.
- Daily written notes; weekly synthesis into artifacts.

## Team Organization (2 people)
- Lead A (Founder): Partnerships + Legal discovery + Adoption process mapping.
- Lead B (Co‑lead): Market/desk research + Data consolidation + Content/knowledge base mapping.
- Shared: Interviewing, synthesis, and documentation.

## Workstreams (Prioritized)

### 1) Market & Ecosystem Research (Serbia) — Highest Priority
- Map actors: shelters, rescuers, foster networks, adopters, donors, vets/clinics, suppliers.
- Quantify needs: funding gaps, common medical issues, food/medicine requirements.
- Benchmarks: successful orgs/portals (features, transparency, adoption tooling).
- Outputs: Serbia Landscape Report, Personas/Journeys, Competitor/Comparable matrix.

### 2) Legal & Compliance (Serbia) — Highest Priority
- Registration path and timing; fundraising/solicitation rules; required disclosures.
- Adoption rules: required contracts, consent, data capture, photos/videos.
- Payments: permitted processors, recurring terms, receipts, tax letters.
- Privacy: GDPR handling of media/evidence, updates, public timelines.
- Outputs: Legal Checklist, Draft policies (Privacy, TOS/AUP, Adoption/Virtual Adoption terms).

### 3) Partnerships & Vet Network — High Priority
 - Use warm introductions only (no cold outreach). Start with people we know who love dogs; ask for referrals and recommendations to shelters, individuals, programs/projects, and vets.
 - Maintain a referral graph (who introduced whom) to prioritize trusted connections and track momentum.
 - Define eligibility and KYC checklist; prepare MOU drafts (update cadence, evidence standards for donor follow‑ups).
 - Vet/clinic directory via introductions: coverage, services, pricing; ambulance services.
 - Outputs: Partner Pipeline built via warm intros, 10–20 pilot shelters, 5–10 vet contacts, draft MOUs.

### 4) Adoption & Virtual Adoption Mapping — High Priority
- Current adoption funnels; bottlenecks; best practices.
- Virtual adoption expectations: update cadence, visit policies, evidence.
- Outputs: Adoption SOP v0, Virtual Adoption SOP v0, consent templates outline.

### 5) Payments & Financial Flows — High Priority
- Specific-case donations and subscriptions; disbursement workflows.
- Follow‑up obligations linked to disbursements; evidence requirements.
- Outputs: Flow diagrams, draft accounting policy implications.

### 6) Content & Knowledge Base — Medium Priority
- Topic taxonomy: care guides, training, legal, health, costs, before-you-adopt.
- Editorial standards; media rights and consent for updates.
- Outputs: Topic map, editorial calendar v0, content sourcing plan.

### 7) Risk, Trust & Transparency — Medium Priority
- Risk register; KYC policy; update evidence verification thresholds.
- Public transparency stats: follow‑up rate, time‑to‑first‑update, verification rate.
- Outputs: Risk Register v0, Transparency Framework v0.

### 8) Metrics & Analytics — Medium Priority
- Define transparency metrics and funnel dashboards needed at launch.
- Outputs: Metric taxonomy v0; dashboard spec outline.

### 9) Technology Scoping — Lower Priority (De‑risking only)
- Compare Shopify/WooCommerce/custom; CMS/CRM/Payments options.
- Data model outline for entities and history timelines (no build yet).
- Outputs: Tech Options memo; draft data model sketch.

## Cadence & Deliverables
- Daily: 1–2 pages of notes per person (facts, sources, highlights).
- Twice-weekly: 30–45 min sync; update `docs/README.md` status.
- Weekly: Synthesis publish — update execution files under `docs/execution-plan/detailed-plan/`.
- Outreach method note: We rely on warm introductions and referral chains (no cold outreach). Track intros in the Partner Pipeline.

## 30–60–90 Day Plan (Investigation‑First)
- Days 1–30: Interviews (15–25), legal discovery, partner pipeline, landscape report v1.
- Days 31–60: Draft policies/SOPs, transparency framework, GTM inputs, financial assumptions v1.
- Days 61–90: Validate with pilots (paper pilots), finalize v1 business docs, decide tech stack.

## Risks & Mitigations
- Access to stakeholders → Warm intros via community, vets, universities.
- Regulatory ambiguity → Conservative defaults, counsel review.
- Bias in samples → Diversify interviewees; triangulate data.
