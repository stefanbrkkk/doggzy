# Execution Plan — 12 Risk, Fraud Prevention & Trust

## Summary
Build risk register, fraud prevention, dispute/refund policy, incident comms, and transparency artifacts. Include verification of update evidence, audit of per‑entity history timelines, and publication of transparency statistics.

## Objectives & Outcomes
- Risk register and heat map.
- Fraud prevention program (KYC, document checks for shelters/posters), review queues.
- Verified transparency: per‑entity history timelines, donor follow‑up logs, and public statistics.

## Scope & Boundaries
- Cross‑functional risk and trust.

## Dependencies
- Legal, Product, Ops, Data.

## Execution Plan (High‑Level)
1) Draft risk register and owners (fraud, privacy, compliance, reputational).
2) Define KYC and fraud controls; implement workflows and audit trails.
3) Define update evidence verification process (required docs/photos, vet confirmations where applicable), and moderation guidelines.
4) Implement audits for history timelines (random sampling, tamper checks, access control reviews).
5) Build transparency pages and stats (follow‑up rates, time‑to‑update, verification rate), with clear definitions.
6) Create incident comms playbook (update failures, evidence disputes, data issues).

## Milestones & Timeline
- Register v1
- Controls implemented
- Evidence verification live
- Transparency stats live
- Playbook approved

## Risks & Mitigations
- False positives → Human‑in‑the‑loop review.
- Privacy leaks in updates → Redaction tools and consent checks.
- Manipulation of timelines → Immutable logs/versioning and access controls.

## Metrics & KPIs
- Fraud rate; dispute rate; time to resolution.
- % updates with verified evidence; % donations with follow‑up; median time‑to‑first‑update; audit pass rate.
