# Execution Plan — 03 Market & Ecosystem Research

## Summary
Research dog welfare and adoption ecosystem in Serbia first: shelters, donors, adopters, vets/clinics, suppliers; benchmarks, regulations.

## Objectives & Outcomes
- Serbia landscape report (shelters, rescuers, adopters, vet coverage, suppliers).
- Donor/adopter personas and journeys.
- Regulatory notes for fundraising and adoption.

## Scope & Boundaries
- Serbia focus; note expansion framework.

## Dependencies
- Legal/Compliance for regulatory confirmation.

## Execution Plan (High‑Level)
1) Desk research: public sources, NGO reports, gov stats.
2) 10–15 expert interviews (shelters, vets, rescuers).
3) Persona mapping: donors, adopters, posters, virtual adopters.
4) Sizing and pain points; benchmark comparables.
5) Publish Serbia v1; identify data gaps.
