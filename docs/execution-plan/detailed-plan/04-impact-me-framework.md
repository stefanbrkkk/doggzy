# Execution Plan — 04 Impact & M&E Framework

## Summary
Define theory of change, KPIs, data collection, verification, and reporting including adoption and virtual adoption outcomes, plus transparent entity histories and donor follow‑ups.

## Objectives & Outcomes
- Logical framework covering donations, shop profit, adoption and virtual adoption.
- KPI catalog and data dictionary, including donor follow‑up KPIs and entity history coverage.
- Reporting cadence and dashboards (public and internal) with per‑entity timelines and statistics.

## Scope & Boundaries
- Program impact, not financial accounting specifics.

## Dependencies
- Product data model; Ops SOPs; Legal (privacy/consent for updates/evidence).

## Execution Plan (High‑Level)
1) Draft theory of change covering donations → activities → outcomes → impact (incl. adoption/virtual adoption).
2) Define KPIs and methods, including:
   - % donations with follow‑up; median time‑to‑first‑update; cadence adherence; % updates with verified evidence
   - % dogs/shelters with active timeline; median updates per entity; evidence attachment rate
3) Specify data collection points in product/ops (timeline event schema, donation→update linkage, verification status).
4) Design dashboards: public per‑entity stats + internal M&E dashboards; define reporting cadence.
5) Verification plan (spot checks, vet attestations, receipts sampling).

## Milestones & Timeline
- ToC drafted
- KPI catalog and data dictionary approved
- Data spec complete (events, linkages, verification fields)
- Dashboards v1 (public + internal)

## Risks & Mitigations
- Uncollectible data → Adjust KPIs to feasible proxies.
- Privacy/consent gaps → Legal review; clear consents.

## Metrics & KPIs
- Dashboard adoption; on‑time reporting.
- Follow‑up rate; time‑to‑first‑update; entity timeline completeness.
