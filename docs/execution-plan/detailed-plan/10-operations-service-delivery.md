# Execution Plan — 10 Operations & Service Delivery

## Summary
Stand up shelter/vet onboarding, adoption and virtual adoption SOPs, donation disbursements, logistics, support, and content ops. Implement transparent per‑entity history timelines and donor follow‑up processes with SLAs and evidence standards.

## Objectives & Outcomes
- Onboarding playbooks; SOPs for disbursements, adoption, virtual adoption.
- Update obligations and donor follow‑up SOPs (cadence, content, evidence, verification).
- Support SLAs and moderation; knowledge base governance.

## Scope & Boundaries
- Ops execution for Serbia launch.

## Dependencies
- Legal terms, Product workflows, Partnerships.

## Execution Plan (High‑Level)
1) Write onboarding and SOP docs; train partners.
   - Include update obligations (what/when/how), consent, and evidence standards for history timelines and donor follow‑ups.
2) Set SLAs, queues, and escalation paths.
   - Define time‑to‑first‑update after donation, recurring cadence for subscriptions/virtual adoption, and missed‑SLA handling.
3) Stand up logistics (in‑kind, shop fulfillment if applicable).
4) Content ops and editorial calendar; define review/approval process for updates and evidence.
5) Configure donor communication templates (email/SMS/push) and notification preferences.

## Milestones & Timeline
- Playbooks
- SOPs (including update/follow‑up)
- Training complete
- Ops go‑live

## Risks & Mitigations
- Fraud or misuse → KYC, review queues, audit trails, evidence verification.
- Missed updates → Automated reminders, escalation, temporary funding holds for non‑compliance.

## Metrics & KPIs

- % donations with follow‑up; median time‑to‑first‑update; cadence adherence; evidence verification rate.
