# One‑Pager (Draft)

## Mission
Transparent, specific‑impact support for dogs in Serbia and beyond: donations (with donor follow‑ups), adoption and virtual adoption, a trusted knowledge base, and a shop whose 100% profit funds care.

## The Problem
- Fragmented adoption information; limited guidance for posters and adopters.
- Funding gaps for shelters/rescuers and low donor transparency.
- Limited access to affordable vet care/ambulances and supplies.

## Our Solution
- Serbia‑first portal that combines:
  - Donations (general and specific to shelters/dogs), including subscriptions.
  - Adoption support and virtual adoption (regular updates and optional visits).
  - Knowledge base + vet/clinic directory (country‑by‑country, starting with Serbia).
  - Shop where 100% of profit funds care (including our own products over time).
- Radical transparency: public histories per dog/shelter/case + donor follow‑ups showing how funds were used.

## Why Now
- Donors expect transparency and ongoing proof of impact.
- Digital workflows enable verified updates (receipts, vet attestations).

## How It Works (Pilot)
1) Onboard 10–20 shelters and 5–10 clinics (Serbia).
2) Launch donations (incl. recurring) and adoption/virtual adoption listings.
3) Publish updates: photos/videos/receipts; vet confirmations where applicable.
4) Share public timelines and stats; notify donors automatically.

## Trust & Accountability
- KPIs we track publicly: % donations with follow‑up, median time‑to‑first‑update, cadence adherence, % verified evidence.
- Update SLAs in MOUs/T&Cs; moderation + verification.

## Business Model
- Donations + subscriptions (incl. virtual adoption).
- Shop profit fully allocated to mission.
- Grants, sponsors, and in‑kind.

## Roadmap (First 12 Months)
- 0–3 mo: Investigation, pilots, policies/SOPs, MVP scope.
- 4–6 mo: MVP build + pilot launch (donations, adoption, transparency v1).
- 7–12 mo: Scale partners and recurring base; first proprietary products.

## Call to Action
- Pilot shelters/vets and advisors (legal/impact/ops) — get in touch.
- Early donors and creators — help us prove the model in Serbia.
