# Executive Summary (Draft)

## Mission & Vision
Enable transparent, specific-impact support for dogs in Serbia and beyond: donations (with donor follow-ups), adoption and virtual adoption, a trusted knowledge base, and a shop whose 100% profit funds care.

## Problem
- Fragmented adoption info and limited support for posters/adopters.
- Funding gaps for shelters and independent rescuers; low donor transparency.
- Limited access to vets/ambulances and affordable supplies.

## Our Solution
- A Serbia-first portal combining:
  - Donations (general and specific to shelters/dogs), including subscriptions.
  - Adoption support and virtual adoption (sponsor a specific dog with regular updates and optional visits).
  - Knowledge base (country-by-country, starting with Serbia) and vet/clinic directory.
  - Shop for dog/owner accessories where 100% of profit funds the mission (including our own products over time).
- Radical transparency with per-entity history timelines and donor follow-ups showing how funds were used.

## Why Now
- Rising expectations for transparency in giving; growth in recurring support.
- Digital tools to verify updates (receipts, vet attestations); growing creator/partner ecosystems.

## Business Model
- Donations and recurring subscriptions (including virtual adoption).
- Shop profit (including proprietary products) fully allocated to the mission.
- Grants, corporate sponsorships, and in-kind partnerships.

## Serbia-First GTM
- Pilot with 10–20 shelters and 5–10 vets/clinics.
- Localized content, language, currency, and compliance.
- Creator/partner campaigns; SEO via knowledge base.

## Transparency & Trust
- Public histories and statistics for each dog/shelter/case.
- KPIs: % donations with follow-up, median time-to-first-update, cadence adherence, % verified evidence.
- Update SLAs in MOUs and T&Cs; verification paths (receipts, vet confirmations).

## Impact Model (High-Level)
Inputs (donations, shop profit) → Activities (funding, supplies, vet care, adoption enablement) → Outputs (dogs treated/fed/adopted) → Outcomes (health, time-to-care, adoption retention) → Impact.

## Roadmap & Milestones (First 12 Months)
- Months 0–3: Investigation-first; pilot partners; policy/SOP drafts; MVP scope.
- Months 4–6: MVP build; pilot launch (donations + adoption + transparency v1).
- Months 7–12: Scale content, partners, and recurring base; introduce first own products.

## Risks & Mitigations
- Compliance ambiguity → Counsel review and conservative defaults.
- Partner follow-up gaps → SLAs, training, reminders, and escalation.
- Fraud/reputation risk → KYC, evidence verification, audits.

## The Ask
- Pilot shelters/vets; donor champions; advisors (legal/impact/ops); initial funding for setup and pilots.
